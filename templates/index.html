CONCEPTS

Entropy

Estimates the uncertainty of a password based on its length and character space.

Hashing

Converts input into a fixed-length representation that cannot practically be reversed.

Salting

Adds a unique random value before hashing to prevent identical passwords from producing identical hashes.
